# Overview

The TBBD HR Portal compliance framework establishes the controls required to support secure, reliable, and responsible operation of the platform.

The framework covers:

- Identity governance
- Data protection
- Security monitoring
- Information governance
- AI responsibility
- Operational controls
- Audit readiness

The compliance approach follows Microsoft security and governance best practices.

---

# Compliance Objectives

The compliance framework aims to:

- Protect employee and business information
- Maintain secure cloud operations
- Support privacy requirements
- Enable audit visibility
- Reduce technology risks
- Establish accountability
- Promote continuous improvement

---

# Compliance Principles

## Security by Design

Security controls are integrated throughout the platform lifecycle.

Practices:

- Secure architecture
- Identity-based access
- Encryption
- Monitoring
- Threat protection

---

## Privacy by Design

Privacy protection is considered during system design and operation.

Practices:

- Data minimization
- Access restrictions
- Secure processing
- Retention management
- Privacy awareness

---

## Compliance by Design

Compliance requirements are incorporated into:

- Architecture decisions
- Development processes
- Deployment procedures
- Operational activities
